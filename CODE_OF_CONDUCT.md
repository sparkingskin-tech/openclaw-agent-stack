# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Welcoming feedback and different viewpoints
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Contact

For reports or concerns, open a private issue or contact project maintainers through the organization profile.
